TRACKING

Domestic and international tracking refers to monitoring website visitors based on their geographic location—whether they are from within your own country (domestic) or from other countries (international).
This tracking is usually done using analytics tools that collect data such as IP address, country, state, city, language, device type, and user behavior.

Domestic tracking focuses on understanding local audience behavior, including which states or cities generate the most traffic, preferred language, peak activity time, and conversion rates. It helps businesses optimize regional marketing campaigns and improve local user experience.

International tracking, on the other hand, analyzes traffic coming from different countries and continents.
It helps identify global audience trends, time zone differences, currency preferences, and performance issues like page load speed. This data supports international SEO, content localization, and global marketing strategies. Together, both types of tracking help businesses make data-driven decisions and expand effectively.
